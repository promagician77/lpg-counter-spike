What you're seeing

Bounding boxes and track IDs

Each cylinder gets a colored box with its track ID and size class. The color stays consistent per track so you can follow one cylinder across frames.

Counting line

The green horizontal line. A cylinder is counted once when its center crosses from above to below. The "armed" state machine prevents jitter from causing double counts.

Occlusion handling

Press "Simulate occlusion" to see two cylinders overlap. ByteTrack keeps their IDs stable through short disappearances — watch the track IDs stay the same.

Size classification

Three classes: 20lb (blue, short), 30lb (silver, medium), 100lb (white, tall). The model classifies visually, cross-checked by pixel height ratio from a calibrated camera.

Cashier Touchscreen

The interface on the touchscreen next to the lane. The cashier sees the AI's count, adjusts any size with + / −, confirms, and the audit trail records both numbers.

Engineering Notes

Decisions, trade-offs, and questions — written before seeing the plant.

Why I didn't train a model yet

A model trained on stock photos of cylinders will fail at your plant. It needs your actual cylinders, under your lighting, including the dirty and damaged ones you mentioned. I'd spend the first few days collecting 200-300 images on-site.

Occlusion strategy

ByteTrack keeps stable IDs through short disappearances (3-5 frames). For longer occlusions I'd train the model with partially visible cylinders as a separate augmentation class, and fall back to the height-ratio check.

Camera placement

The camera should be perpendicular to the cylinder flow, not angled. An angle compresses depth and makes occlusion worse. A fixed mount at a known distance also makes size calibration reliable.

Night and weather

The Bahamas has strong sun and sudden rain. I'd use an IP67 camera with IR capability, and train the model with brightness/contrast augmentation. An IR illuminator for dawn/dusk conditions.

QuickBooks integration

Proposed separately as you said. For Desktop it's the QuickBooks SDK (COM-based); for Online it's the REST API with OAuth. I'd need to know which version before scoping.

Scaling to more lanes

Phase 1 is one lane. Adding a second is one more camera and one more counter on the same Jetson. The Orin Nano can handle two 640×640 streams at ~15 fps each, or a second Jetson for full speed.

Questions for you

Truck flow

Does one truck arrive, get counted, and leave? Or continuous flow? This decides whether the counter resets per truck.

Camera position

Overhead, side-mounted, or at the gate? Shapes the model's training perspective.

Accuracy target

95% means 1 in 20 wrong. 99% is much harder. The cashier UI covers the gap, but the target helps.

QuickBooks version

Desktop or Online? Decides Phase 2 approach.
